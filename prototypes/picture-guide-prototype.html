Settled, and what is still open

Your six answers are baked into the rendering above. Three things are left.

A. Do the example photos exist as image files?

If they only live inside the printed sheets, pulling roughly 150 usable images back out of them is its own job and worth scoping before the build starts. If they came from a folder of photographs originally, this part is nothing.

B. Repeatable shots — is a counter right?

The printout writes 4+ and 12+ where a lister may need several: three LCD flaws, two accessories. I gave those chips a −/+ counter so the numbering stays exact all the way down the board. If nobody would bother setting it in practice, I will print the + instead and leave the rest nominal, like the paper does.

Try it — turn LCD Flaws up to 3 and watch everything after it shift.
C. Should the Station view remember where it got to?

It walks the run one shot at a time, but nothing is stored. If two listers share a station, or someone walks away mid-item, a remembered position is a stale position.

My recommendation: no memory. A half-ticked list from someone else’s item is worse than none.
