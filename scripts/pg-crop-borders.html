Picture Guide — strip the baked-in borders

Photos taken out of the printed guide carry a border drawn into the image itself: a 3px line with a 1px antialias fringe outside it, and on the red ones a second fringe inside too, so black borders are 4px and red ones are 5px. The app draws its own frame and its own OPTIONAL flag, so that border lands as a second outline just inside the first. This finds the border on each photo, trims it off, and puts the trimmed photo back under the same filename — nothing in the database changes. Edit NAMES below for a new batch.
